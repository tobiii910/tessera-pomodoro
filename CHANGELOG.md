# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First version: a pomodoro timer with focus, a short break and a long break (25, 5 and 15 minutes, the long break
  after every fourth round), all set in the plugin's settings. Start, Pause, Skip and Reset on the tile and the card,
  the rounds as dots, a popup when a phase ends, and the running pomodoro in the top bar. It keeps running through a
  restart of the screen.
